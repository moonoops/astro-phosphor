## Overview

**Trail Map** demonstrates Astro Phosphor's static publishing workflow under `/journal`. Articles are authored as typed Astro Content Collection MDX entries with build-time Shiki syntax highlighting, heading extraction, and client-side tag/category/keyword filtering.

## Publishing Workflow

- **Strict Content Schemas:** Frontmatter validated by Zod at build time (`src/content.config.ts`) so broken dates or missing metadata never ship.
- **Bilingual CJK Support:** Dedicated `lang: zh-CN` typography spacing, optical line heights, and fallback CJK font stacks across all 5 OSes.
- **Reading Mode:** One click softens CRT scanlines, edge vignette, glass glare, and barrel curvature for calm long-form reading.
